# 📜 Version History (Changelog)

All important changes and updates to the **Lunar/Solar/Planetary Autofocus V2** plugin for FireCapture are documented here.

---

## [2.9] - 2026-10-03
### 🚀 New Features & Enhancements
* **Dual Dropdown Step Configuration:** Replaced the manual text field with two dedicated dropdown menus for **Step Size** (`5, 10, 15, 20`) and **Steps Count** (`10, 15, 20`) to precisely and safely control the scanning range in the field.
* **Dynamic & Symmetric Autofocus Routine & Backlash Optimization:** Automated calculation of the scan radius based on the user's step configuration, paired with an optimized mechanical backlash recovery sequence (reversing by radius + a reduced safety margin of **50 steps**) to prevent excessive out-of-focus excursions, preserve FireCapture's auto-alignment tracking, and guarantee absolute symmetry and a perfectly centered V-Curve[cite: 7].
* **Real-time Scan Progress Counter:** Added a live step counter (`Step X/total`) directly in the status display, fully localized across all 12 supported languages[cite: 7].
* **GitHub Update Checker:** Integrated automated online version checking with secure TLS protocol handling to notify users and simplify downloads for future updates directly from the GUI[cite: 7].

## [2.8] - 2026-10-01
### 🎯 Seeing Optimization & Smart Sub-ROI
* **"Difficult Seeing" Mode:** Introduced the new advanced option featuring *Smart Stacking & Robust Fit* to stabilize and improve sharpness analysis even under poor seeing conditions[cite: 7].
* **Active (Anchored) Sub-ROI:** Implemented support for the anchored software calculation sub-area, allowing precise millimeter-level isolation of planetary or lunar targets[cite: 7].
* **Stability & Interface:** Further optimizations for the V2.8 build and real-time calculation workflows[cite: 7].

## [2.7] - 2026-09-29
### ✨ New Features & Interface
* **Moon/Sun Mode:** Renamed and expanded the classic "Moon" option to **"Moon/Sun"** to officially reflect full compatibility and optimization for solar imaging (H-alpha and white light), complete with localization across all 12 supported languages[cite: 7].
* **Dynamic UI Update:** Real-time synchronization of the calculation mode dropdown menu when switching languages on-the-fly directly from the GUI[cite: 7].

## [2.6] - 2026-09-29
### 🚀 Advanced Optimization & Lucky Autofocus
* **Lucky Autofocus Anti-Seeing Algorithm:** Revolutionized step sampling by introducing multi-frame capture during the stabilization pause. The plugin analyzes the stream and automatically selects the maximum score (the frame where seeing momentarily stabilized)[cite: 7].
* **Dedicated Settle Time Selector:** Added a dropdown menu for custom configuration of focuser stabilization time and Lucky AF sampling window (with selectable intervals from 500ms to 3000ms)[cite: 7].

## [2.5] - 2026-09-29
### 🛠️ Architecture & Stability
* **Version Update & Structure:** Consolidation of the V2.5 build with background calculation thread optimization and seamless integration of multilingual graphical components (12 languages)[cite: 7].

## [2.3] - 2026-09-29
### 🚀 New Features
* **Automatic CSV Export:** Added automatic saving of all V-Curve points and data to a `.csv` file inside the system's `home` directory (`user.home`) upon completion of the autofocus routine[cite: 7].
* **Dummy Cam Compatibility:** The log export system is fully operational even during bench simulations with the virtual camera (*dummy cam*)[cite: 7].

## [2.2] - 2026-07-xx
### ⚡ Optimizations & Performance
* **Settle Time / Throttling Management:** Introduced temporal control over frame sampling (e.g., adjustable intervals at 500ms or 1s). Reduces calculation load while keeping FireCapture's video stream 100% fluid[cite: 7].

## [2.1] - 2026-05-xx
### 🛠️ Improvements & Stability
* **ASCOM Driver Reset:** Optimized focuser management and initialization procedure to prevent hardware communication lockups[cite: 7].
* **Interface and Localization:** Perfected dynamic Sub-ROI selection and stabilized multilingual support (12 languages)[cite: 7].

## [2.0] - 2026-03-xx (First Stable Release)
### ✨ Initial Release
* **V-Curve Engine:** Implementation of least squares parabola calculation ($y = ax^2 + bx + c$) for high-precision focus vertex detection ($X_v = -b/2a$)[cite: 7].
* **Dual Calculation Mode:**
  * *Moon Mode:* Absolute spatial gradient analysis ($dx, dy$) pixel by pixel[cite: 7].
  * *Planet Mode:* Hybrid weighted analysis with normalized average intensity to isolate planetary details while reducing seeing impact[cite: 7].
* **Native Integration:** Direct communication with FireCapture's focuser instance via `SystemSettings.getCurrent().getFocuser()`[cite: 7].
